## Goal-Writing Reference

A well-formed IEP goal has 4 parts:
1. **Condition** — In what setting or context?
2. **Student name** — always use the student's name
3. **Behaviour** — what specific, observable behaviour?
4. **Criteria** — how well, how often, by when?

**Example (Communication):**
> During structured classroom activities, Ahmad Izzat will use PECS Phase 4 sentence strip "I want ___" to request a preferred item with 80% accuracy across 3 consecutive sessions by July 2026.

**Example (Behaviour):**
> When feeling overwhelmed (as signalled by teacher), Hazwan will independently move to the calm corner (without physical guidance) on 3 out of 5 opportunities by October 2026.
