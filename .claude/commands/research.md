# /research [name] [question] — Evidence-Based Research for a Specific Student

Research a strategy or intervention grounded in THIS student's documented profile.

## Steps

1. Read the student's full profile (`students/S*-[name].md`)
2. Read `memory/malaysia-resources.md` for Malaysian context
3. Identify which aspects of the student's profile are most relevant to the question
4. Research the question with minimum 3 web searches
5. Write findings directly grounded in the student's profile — not generic autism advice

## Research approach

- **Lead with evidence**: peer-reviewed studies, clinical guidelines, established frameworks (PECS, ABA, OT, AAC)
- **Malaysian first**: look for Malaysian or regional guidelines before international ones; international is fine when local not available
- **Counter-evidence**: always check if there is evidence this approach might NOT work for this profile
- **Check the student's profile**: if a strategy has already been tried and stopped, flag it — do not re-recommend
- **Level of certainty**: be honest — "strong evidence", "emerging evidence", "anecdotal/community consensus", "no strong evidence found"

## Output format

```
📖 RESEARCH — [Student Name]: [Question]
*Profile loaded: [ASD Level, Communication: PECS Ph. X / AAC / etc.]*

SUMMARY: [2–3 sentence answer specific to this student]

---
WHAT THE EVIDENCE SAYS:
[Findings, grounded in the student's profile. Cite sources. 3–5 key points max.]

RELEVANT TO THIS STUDENT SPECIFICALLY:
[How does this student's sensory profile / communication level / learning profile affect which approach to take?]

WHAT TO TRY FIRST:
[1–3 concrete, actionable suggestions. Specific to this student — not generic.]

WHAT PROBABLY WON'T WORK (for this student):
[Based on their profile, what should be avoided? Be specific — don't just say "every child is different".]

MALAYSIAN RESOURCES:
[Any Malaysian-specific programme, therapist type, or organisation relevant to this question]

SOURCES:
- [URL 1]
- [URL 2]

---
⚠️ Verify with your OT/SLT/SERC before implementing any new strategy.
Research here is a starting point — your professional team has direct knowledge of this child.
```

## Notes for Claude
- If the question is about medication, supplements, or medical treatment: do NOT research this. Say: "Medication and medical treatment questions should go directly to the student's paediatrician or psychiatrist."
- If the question is about something the profile shows has already been tried and stopped, surface that first: "The profile shows [strategy] was tried and stopped in [period]. Is this question about why it stopped, or looking for an alternative?"
- Always end with the verify caveat — this is mandatory, not optional
