## 4. Behavioural Profile

### 🟡 Meltdown early warning signs
**Physical signs:**
- Hand-flapping increases significantly in speed
- Begins rocking in seat (faster than usual self-soothing rock)
- Covers both ears with palms

**Behavioural signs:**
- Abandons current task, stands up suddenly
- Moves toward door

**Vocal signs:**
- High-pitched whimper (distinct from his usual vocalisations)

### 🔴 De-escalation — what works for Izzat
1. Guide him to the calm corner with a gentle gesture toward it (do NOT carry or push)
2. Place weighted blanket on the chair/floor nearby — let him reach for it himself
3. Sit at floor level, 1 metre away. No eye contact. No talking.
4. Wait — average recovery: 10–15 minutes. Do not rush.
5. Once calm (sitting quietly, rocking slows): offer preferred activity (iPad, puzzle) before returning to class work

### ❌ Makes it WORSE for Izzat
- Direct eye contact while distressed — increases agitation
- Physical touch / holding during meltdown — escalates to head-hitting
- Asking questions or giving instructions
- Other students crowding around
- Raising voice

### ⚠️ Crisis / Emergency Protocol
1. Remove objects that can be thrown (books, pencils, containers)
2. Elopement: Stand near door with visual STOP card — do NOT block doorway physically
3. Other students: Send to Library Room with aide if available; face them away otherwise
4. Head-hitting: Offer the wall-press alternative. Do not restrain unless injury is imminent.
5. Call Puan Siti Hajar (mother: 012-3456789) if crisis exceeds 20 minutes or involves any injury
6. If injury occurs: complete school incident report form; send copy to parent
7. Log in incidents.md immediately after

**Elopement protocol detail:** Izzat runs toward the classroom door. He does not go far — typically stops in the corridor. Assign aide to follow at distance (do not chase — increases panic). Use "finish" PECS card to indicate safe endpoint.
