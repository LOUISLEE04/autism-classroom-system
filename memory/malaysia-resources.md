## National Organisations

### NASOM — National Autism Society of Malaysia
- Website: nasom.org.my
- Services: Early intervention, therapy, school support, parent training
- State branches: KL (HQ), Selangor, Penang, Johor, Sabah, Sarawak, others
- Phone (HQ): 03-7956 4929
- For teachers: Can consult for specific student challenges; offer training workshops

### Malaysian Association for Children with Learning Disabilities (MACLD)
- Website: macld.org.my
- Focus: Learning disabilities including autism
- Resources: Parent guides, educator guides, therapy referrals

### Persatuan Kanak-kanak Istimewa Malaysia (PKIM)
- Services: Community support, legal advocacy for families of special needs children

## Government Programmes

### PPKI — Program Pendidikan Khas Integrasi
- What it is: Special education integration programme in mainstream government schools
- Ministry of Education (KPM) under Bahagian Pendidikan Khas (BPK)
- Contact: BPK KPM — 03-8884 9000
- Resources: Teacher training, curriculum guides, assessment frameworks
- URL: moe.gov.my → Pendidikan Khas

### SERC — Special Education Resource Centre
- What it is: State-level resource centres providing support for special ed teachers
- Each state has a SERC — contact via JPN (Jabatan Pendidikan Negeri)
- Can provide: Visiting consultants, materials, professional development
- **Teacher should identify their nearest SERC contact and add it to teacher-profile.md**

### JKM — Jabatan Kebajikan Masyarakat
- What it is: Department of Social Welfare — funds programmes and aids families
- Services relevant to students: OKU registration, OKU card benefits, disability allowance
- Portal: ejkm.jkm.gov.my
- OKU registration: Required for students to access JKM benefits and school support
- Phone: 03-8000 8000

### Permata
- Early childhood intervention programme (government)
- For younger children (preschool to early primary)
- Can be referenced for students who received early intervention there

## Therapy & Clinical Services

### Occupational Therapy (OT)
- Available at: Hospital Universiti Malaya (PPUM), Hospital Kuala Lumpur, private clinics
- KKM (Ministry of Health) referral: GP/paediatrician referral for public hospital OT
- Private OT: Typically RM80–180 per session
- Waitlists for public OT can be 6–12 months — recommend private for urgent cases

### Speech-Language Therapy (SLT)
- Available at: PPUM, Hospital KL, selected district hospitals, private clinics
- School-based SLT: Some schools have visiting SLT — check with SERC
- Private SLT: RM100–200 per session
- For non-verbal students: SLT assessment essential before PECS phase advancement

### Applied Behaviour Analysis (ABA)
- Providers: NASOM centres, private ABA clinics
- Note: Quality varies — ask for board-certified behaviour analysts (BCBA)
- School collaboration: ABA therapist can observe classroom and support teacher strategies

## Communication Systems

### PECS (Picture Exchange Communication System)
- Training: PECS training available through NASOM and private providers
- Materials: Can be printed (do-it-yourself) or purchased; symbol libraries (e.g., Boardmaker, Widgit)
- Phase guide:
  - Phase 1: Exchanges single picture to get desired item
  - Phase 2: Distance/persistence — seeks out communication partner
  - Phase 3: Discrimination between symbols
  - Phase 4: Sentence structure "I want ___"
  - Phase 5: Responds to "What do you want?"
  - Phase 6: Comments, answers questions

### AAC Systems in Malaysia
- Common apps: Proloquo2Go (iOS), TouchChat, LAMP Words for Life, LetMeTalk (free, Android)
- Hardware: iPad is most common; some students use dedicated speech-generating devices
- Funding: OKU card holders may qualify for subsidy — check JKM
- Training: SLT should train both teacher and parents on AAC use

### Bahasa Isyarat Malaysia (BIM)
- Malaysia's official sign language
- Not the same as Makaton (Makaton is used in some special schools — simpler subset)
- Resources: OKU Forum Malaysia, Jabatan Kebajikan Masyarakat

## Malaysian Education Law & Policy

### Special Education Regulation 2013 (Peraturan-peraturan Pendidikan (Pendidikan Khas) 2013)
- Governs the right to special education in Malaysia
- Key points: Students eligible for PPKI must be assessed by a licensed professional; placement requires formal documentation

### OKU Act (Persons with Disabilities Act 2008)
- Establishes rights of persons with disabilities including children
- Relevant for teacher advocacy when requesting school resources
